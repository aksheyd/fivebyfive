How to play

Pick an empty seat next to a card, then guess the card you’ll draw.

  • One neighbor Higher or lower than it.
  • Two neighbors Inside or outside their range.
  • Three or four Same suit as one of them, or not.

Ties count for you, and aces are low. A miss discards the drawn card, sends that row and column under the deck, and deals the middle row again. Fill all 25 seats to win.
